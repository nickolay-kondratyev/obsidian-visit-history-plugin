After a sync most files share one mtime. When .git is a folder at the vault root, the clustered-modified-times banner offers 'Restore from git' (also command restore-modified-times-from-git). Walks history from HEAD with isomorphic-git over a read-only DataAdapter fs; writes the same bytes back with the commit time only after the walk and only for files still matching HEAD. Adds isomorphic-git + buffer (+175 KB bundle). Ticket nid_z3ifk9vxm295cpu4tqu99gj7m_e.
